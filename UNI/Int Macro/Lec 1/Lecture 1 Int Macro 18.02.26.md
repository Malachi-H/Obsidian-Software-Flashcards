**Real GDP**: Real GDP (Gross Domestic Product) is
the inflation-adjusted value of all final goods and services produced within an economy in a given year, expressed in constant prices - Slide 8

**Real GDP per worker:** represents the total economic output (adjusted for inflation) generated per unit of labour input - Slide 9

**Cash Rate**: RBA targets a cash rate: The cash rate is the interest rate on unsecured overnight loans between banks - Slide 13
