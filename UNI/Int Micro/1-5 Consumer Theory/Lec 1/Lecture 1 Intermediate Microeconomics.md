**Consumer's Economic Circumstances**

Key assumptions in Micro
When modelling micro behaviour we will assume that economic agents are *rational* and in pursuit of their perceived *self interest*
	- Rational: agents seek to do the best given their circumstances they face
	- Self interest: agents make decisions that increase their own welfare

*""“Rationality” and “Self-Interest” are typically assumed in any standard microeconomic model.""*


**Consumer theory**
How do economists model the way we make a choice as consumers?
	- Underlying view is based on two key assumptions of rationality and self interest
		- what is best for us depends on our preferences
		- what is possible depends on economic circumstances

A consumers economics circumstances: Assumptions
	- Assumption 1: Assume the economic circumstances faced by a consumer are entirely described by the *income* of the consumer and the *prices* of the goods
	- Assumption 2: Consumers are *price takers* (they cannot influence the prices of goods)
	- Assumption 3: The income of a consumer is *exogenous*
		- at the moment of making the choice, the consumer has a previously determined income that cannot be modified. (The only choice is how to spend it)
